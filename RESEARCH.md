# Research framing

This project evaluates model behavior under partial observation, limited resources, typed hazards, time pressure, and reproducible action consequences. Current metrics include success, survival, score components, steps and simulated time, provider and engine latency, invalid and repeated actions, action diversity/repetition, exploration coverage, first-discovery steps, milestones, inventory weight, hazard damage, NPC interaction, and resource efficiency. It includes a deliberately small tabular-Q infrastructure baseline, but does not claim intelligence gains or scientific findings.

Built-in comparisons include observation-detail and memory ablations, deterministic cautious/explorer baselines, recovery after failed actions, per-seed benchmark tables, directional benchmark deltas, and replay fingerprint checks across matching scenario versions and seeds.
